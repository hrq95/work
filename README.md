## 🕘 Best Times to Reach Me

- **I'm most responsive and available between 9:00 and 14:00.**
- Outside of these hours, I may be in deep focus mode and responses might be delayed.
- Please plan your communication accordingly and respect this window.

---

## 📬 How to Contact Me

- **Always reach out via private message before calling me.** Unscheduled calls disrupt deep work and I won't answer them.
- Prefer **async communication** — write a clear message with context and I'll get back to you promptly during my available hours.
- The more context you provide upfront, the faster and more effectively I can help.

## ⚡ What Energizes Me

- **Large, complex, self-contained tasks** — give me a hard problem and the space to solve it. I do my best work when I can immerse myself deeply over an extended period.
- **System design and architecture** — I genuinely enjoy thinking about how components interact, how systems scale, and how to build things that last.
- **Technical ownership** — I prefer to understand a problem fully and drive it to completion rather than hand off half-finished work.
- **Clean, purposeful solutions** — I care about quality and take pride in what I deliver.

---

## 🧠 How I Work Best

- I need **uninterrupted focus time** to do my best work. Deep technical problems require sustained concentration.
- I work most effectively when I have **clear goals, minimal interruptions, and full context** upfront.
- I'm highly self-sufficient — once I understand a task, you don't need to check in constantly. I'll reach out if I'm blocked.
- I prefer written communication over verbal — it's clearer, trackable, and respects everyone's time.

---

## 🚫 What Drains Me

- **Meetings that could have been an email.** If the information can be shared in writing, please do so.
- **Status update meetings and recurring check-ins** without a clear technical agenda drain my energy and fragment my focus.
- **Vague, open-ended discussions** that don't lead to actionable outcomes.
- **Unnecessary ceremony and bureaucratic overhead** — I'd rather spend that time shipping something meaningful.
- If my presence at a meeting isn't strictly necessary, I prefer to skip it and stay focused on delivering value.

| Do ✅ | Avoid ❌ |
